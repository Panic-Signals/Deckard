# Phase 6 — Lock snapshots

> Roadmap: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-22-parity-roadmap.md`. Inline, test-first; the
> software and the MK3.

**Goal:** Maschine's Lock: up to 64 snapshots of every modulatable parameter, taken and
recalled with LOCK, managed in Extended Lock (banks, Update, Delete), morphing between
them, recalled from MIDI; stored in the project as Maschine stores them.

**Sources:** the MK3 manual pp. 204–208; `SnapshotsPage.lua`, `SnapshotsHelper.lua`,
`MaschineControllerMK3:onLockButton`; decompiles in `out/decomp/snap`
(`ParameterSnapshotsAccess`, `ParameterSnapshotsManager`, `ParameterSnapshot`); the
snapshots in the user's own projects (read from copies).

## Findings
- **Where:** the Song's `ParameterSnapshotsManager` (every mixing layer has one; only
  the Song's is used): `snapshots`, an `ObjectMap<slot, ParameterSnapshot>` (slot 0–63:
  bank × 16 + pad), and its parameters, stored as `Morph` (bool, off), `Mode` (Travel,
  Target; 0), `Time` (1/2 Bar, 1 Bar, 2 Bars, 4 Bars, 8 Bars, 16 Bars; 1), `Grid` (1/8,
  1/4, 1/2, Bar; 3), the focused bank (0) and the number of banks (1–4, 1).
- **A snapshot** holds the chains' slots (references) and a map from parameter to value
  (as stored). Captured (a 64-sound project: ~1900): every sound's and group's mixer
  strip (level, pan, mute, aux levels, transpose, input gain, audio mute), the swing
  (amount, cycle, invert) of each mixing layer, each slot's on/off, every parameter of
  every module (internal effects and instruments, the Sampler and its envelopes and
  LFO, plug-ins' parameters). A level's entry also names its mute (and the mute's
  value), a mute's its level: Maschine's mute-as-fader pair.
- **LOCK** (`setSnapshotActiveHW(true)`): updates the focused snapshot, or takes a new one
  (the first free slot) when none is focused, and lights LOCK; pressed again
  (`false`): recalls it and unlights. **SHIFT + LOCK**: the Snapshots page (Extended
  Lock): a free pad takes a snapshot there; a pad with one recalls it
  (`triggerParameterSnapshotForPad`, natively); ERASE + pad, or DELETE, deletes; UPDATE
  re-takes the focused one; ◀ ▶ change bank (▶ past the last adds one, up to 4); knobs
  1–3: Morph, Mode, Time (Travel) or Grid (Target).
- **Morphing:** Travel: from now over Time bars; Target: arriving at the next Grid
  boundary (Bar: the next downbeat).
- **MIDI Lock Change** (Edit ▸ MIDI Change): None, MIDI Note or Program Change, a source
  and a channel: note n (0–63) or program n+1 recalls slot n+1.

   parameter, recall restores both; save and reopen keeps it; delete.
3. **Lock in the app** (`App.lock`: the focused slot and LOCK's state): LOCK, Extended

## Rulings
- What a snapshot takes follows the user's Maschine 3 snapshots (mixer strips, swing,
  slot on/off, module parameters); parameters Maschine may take that those projects
  didn't show (Solo, macro values) aren't. If wrong: a recall leaves those as they are.
- A morph glides continuous parameters and switches the rest on arrival, then
  recalls the whole snapshot; its steps are one undo step ("Morph Lock"), the arrival
  another ("Recall Lock"). If wrong: Maschine may switch discrete parameters at the
  start, and morphs may not be undoable there.
- Target with the song stopped arrives after one Grid length. If wrong: Maschine may
  recall at once when stopped.
- MIDI Change's trigger in projects from before Maschine 3 (`MidiRecall` v1): on/off
  and a kind (0 notes, 1 programs), not confirmed from Maschine 2. If wrong: such a
  project's Lock trigger reads and writes the wrong kind.
- Lock's MIDI messages (on its input and channel) don't also play sounds.
- A Lock MIDI source "All" listens to every input (Maschine's list may differ).
